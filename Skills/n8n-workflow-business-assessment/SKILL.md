# Industry fit, reuse and commercial potential

Use after [workflow discovery](../n8n-workflow-discovery/SKILL.md) and
[technical audit](../n8n-workflow-technical-audit/SKILL.md), with the available owner context.
Contribute to the private audit report; do not change workflow JSON or contact potential buyers.

## Fit and reuse

Identify the problem solved, inputs/outcomes and likely operator. Propose plausible industries
based on those capabilities, not just popular sectors. Rank useful matches and name for each:
buyer/team, concrete task, fit now, adaptations required, reusable modules, limitations and
operating burden. Explain why the automation is useful or why the available evidence is weak.
Separate general modules from hardcoded client/provider assumptions. Include meaningful cases
where it should not be reused. Treat time savings, ROI, demand and price ideas as hypotheses
unless supported by actual provided evidence or verified current research.

## Commercial readiness

Assess separately when relevant:
- A reusable template: setup burden, documentation, portability, differentiation and support.
- A client implementation service: requirements discovery, adaptation/testing, handover and scope.
- A managed service: hosting, monitoring, reliability, provider usage costs and ongoing support.

Name blockers and a practical path from the current reference to a usable offering. Quality,
marketability and permission to resell are different questions; a technically promising workflow
is not automatically a finished product or a profitable service.

## Provenance and rights evidence

Inspect owner-supplied source links, author/attribution notes and license information. A public
download is not sufficient evidence of resale permission. If the original source can be identified,
verify its actual license/terms from that source and cite them; do not infer licensing from a
similar template or from n8n's own license. Include relevant dependencies/provider restrictions
only where supported and material to the proposed offering.

If provenance or terms are missing, label resale permission **unknown** and list what must be
resolved. State documented conditions/uncertainty rather than offering blanket legal clearance.
Use current authoritative sources for claims about legal terms, pricing, market conditions or
provider policies. Do not manufacture prices or imply permission through a translated/rebuilt copy.

Finish with industry adaptations and commercial preparation proposals the owner can approve.
