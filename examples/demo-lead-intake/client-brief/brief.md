## What the client asked for

"Every contact-form lead from our website should land in our leads sheet within a minute, and
nobody should have to copy them by hand anymore. Junk (no email) should be ignored." (Demo Co is
fictional. This whole project is a sample.)

## How they do it today

The form emails the office. Someone copies name, email, company and message into a Google Sheet
once a day, so leads wait up to 24 hours.

error back when the email is missing.
